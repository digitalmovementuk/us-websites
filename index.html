Preview terms

What are the preview terms?

The short version. You receive the full written terms before we start building.

  • One private preview per business. It covers every agreed page. Forms on the preview are in test mode, search engines don't index it, and it can't be used as your live website.

  • Unlimited feedback rounds on the agreed pages until you approve.

  • You approve in writing. An email is enough. Not replying doesn't count as approval.

  • Payment of $2,495 only after written approval. Then we send one invoice. After payment, your website goes live and we hand over access, an export and the license inventory.

  • No approval, no invoice. If you don't approve, there is no invoice for the build, and we close the preview. Your own text, photos and domain stay yours.

  • Timing: we confirm it after our first call. We don't promise a delivery date before that.

The offer is made by Digital Movement Marketing Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales. Prices are in US dollars. Page updated October 20, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What do people ask before they start?

Is there really no deposit?

Yes. There is no deposit and no card for the preview. The only invoice is the $2,495, and we send it after you approve your finished website in writing.

How many changes can I ask for?

As many as you like. Feedback rounds are unlimited on the agreed pages until you approve your website. New features or pages outside the agreed scope need their own quote.

What happens if I don't approve the preview?

Then you don't pay for the build. No approval, no invoice: we close the preview. Not replying doesn't count as approval. Your own material, such as your text, photos and domain, stays yours.

Who is this offer for?

Business owners and decision-makers in the United States who need a new website or a redesign of their current one. The form asks two quick questions to check the fit.

Who is behind Digital Movement?

Digital Movement is one team. The US offer is made and invoiced by our UK company, Digital Movement Marketing Ltd. Its directors are Raoul Müller and Martey Quaye, both identity-verified at Companies House.

Can I move my website to another provider later?

Yes. It's a WordPress website, so it can move to another provider. On payment, we transfer the custom work we own and can assign, with a license inventory. WordPress itself and third-party themes, plugins, fonts and images keep their own licenses, and the inventory lists them.

How long does it take?

We confirm the build timing on our first call, once we know your pages and what content you already have. We don't promise a delivery date before that.
