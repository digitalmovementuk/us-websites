How the pay-after preview works

Four steps. You decide before you pay.

  1. Request your preview

    Answer three quick questions in the form in our Facebook or Instagram ad. We call you within 1 business day, during US business hours, to talk through your services, pages and timing.

  2. We build your website first

    Up to seven agreed pages, with writing, mobile layouts and a contact form. We build it privately. Nothing goes live yet.

  3. You review every page

  4. Approve, then pay

One finished WordPress website for your business, for $2,495 one-time.

Questions and answers

Is there really no deposit?

Yes. There is no deposit and no card for the preview. The only invoice is the $2,495, and we send it after you approve your finished website in writing.

What happens if I don't approve the preview?

Then you don't pay for the build. We don't send an invoice, and we close the preview. Not replying doesn't count as approval. Your own material, such as your text, photos and domain, stays yours.

Who is this offer for?

Business owners and decision-makers in the United States who need a new website or a redesign of their current one. The form in our ad asks three quick questions to check the fit.

Can I move my website to another provider later?

Yes. It's a WordPress website, so it can move to another provider. On payment, we transfer the custom work we own and can assign, with a license inventory. WordPress itself and third-party themes, plugins, fonts and images keep their own licenses, and the inventory lists them.

How long does it take?

We confirm the build timing on our first call, once we know your pages and what content you already have. We don't promise a delivery date before that.

Preview terms

The short version. You receive the full written terms before we start building.

  • One private preview per business. It covers every agreed page. Forms on the preview are in test mode, search engines don't index it, and it can't be used as your live website.
  • You approve in writing. An email is enough. Not replying doesn't count as approval.
  • If you approve: we send one invoice for $2,495. After payment, your website goes live and we hand over access, an export and the license inventory.
  • If you don't approve: there is no invoice for the build, and we close the preview. Your own text, photos and domain stay yours.
  • Timing: we confirm it after our first call. We don't promise a delivery date before that.
