Privacy notice

How Digital Movement uses your information

Effective date: October 19, 2026 · Version 1.2, which adds the form on our website and replaces version 1.1 of the same day · For people in the United States who ask us about a website through the form on this website, our Facebook or Instagram form, by email or by phone.

The short version. We collect what you enter in our forms, on this website or in our Facebook or Instagram ad (name, phone, business name, your answers and, if you give it, your email), and use it for one thing: to contact you about your website request and, if you go ahead, to prepare your preview and invoice. We don't sell your information, we don't share it for advertising, and we don't put you on a marketing list. You can ask us to stop contacting you, or to see, correct or delete your information, at any time: email office@digitalmovement.uk or call +44 7865 064463.

Digital Movement Marketing Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17110525), 128 City Road, London EC1V 2NX, United Kingdom. In this notice, "we" and "Digital Movement" mean this company. We are responsible for the information described here.

Contact for anything in this notice: office@digitalmovement.uk, phone +44 7865 064463.

2. What we collect

From the form on this website:

  • Your full name, phone number and business name, and your email address if you choose to give it.
  • Your answers to two questions: whether you are the owner or a decision-maker of the business, and whether the price fits your budget. If you answer "No" to either question, the form closes and nothing is sent.
  • Whether you ticked the box agreeing to calls and texts about your request, the wording of that box, the page address and the date and time you sent the form.

The form sends this information from your browser directly to HubSpot, our customer database, only when you press the button. Nothing is sent while you type. HubSpot also receives the technical details that come with any web request, such as your IP address and browser type.

From the lead form in our Facebook or Instagram ad:

  • Your full name, phone number, email address and business name.
  • Your answers to the form's questions: whether you are the owner or a decision-maker of the business, what you need (a new website, a redesign, or not sure yet), and whether the price fits your budget.
  • Whether you ticked the box agreeing to calls and texts about your request.
  • Details Meta attaches to the submission: the form, ad and lead identifiers, and the date and time you submitted. Meta may also send a one-time code to your phone to confirm the number before you submit.

If you email or call us: whatever you choose to tell us, such as your business, your current website and what you need.

Anything else from this website: nothing. This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising pixels or other trackers. The company that hosts it (GitHub) may keep ordinary server logs, such as the IP address of a request, for its own security purposes under its own terms.

We don't ask for, and please don't send, sensitive information such as Social Security numbers, financial account details, health information or government ID.

3. Why we use it

  • To contact you about your website request, by phone call, text message or email.
  • To check whether the offer fits your business and to agree the pages and scope.
  • To prepare your private preview and, if you approve it, your invoice and handover.
  • To keep a record of your request and of any resulting agreement, for accounting and in case of a dispute.

That is all. We don't add you to a newsletter or marketing list, we don't upload your details to build advertising audiences, and we don't start automated follow-up marketing. A small automation creates a callback reminder for each new request in HubSpot; a person on our team reviews each request and makes the call.

4. Calls and texts

When you tick the box in a form (on this website or in our ad), you agree that Digital Movement may call or text you at the number you gave us about your website request. Our calls and texts are made by a person on our team, not by automated dialing systems, and they are about your request only. Message and data rates may apply to texts. Agreeing is not a condition of buying anything from us.

To stop: reply STOP to any text from us (QUIT, END, CANCEL or UNSUBSCRIBE also work), tell us on a call, or email office@digitalmovement.uk. We stop as soon as we can, and no later than 10 business days after your request. We keep a note of your number only so that we don't contact it again.

Sale and sharing

We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We haven't done either in the last 12 months. We don't knowingly sell or share information about anyone under 16. Because there is no sale or sharing, there is nothing to opt out of, and this website has no "Do Not Sell or Share" link. This website also sets no cookies, so a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser has nothing to switch off here.

Your rights

  • Right to know and access: what personal information we have collected about you, where it came from, why we use it, who we disclosed it to, and a copy of it.
  • Right to delete: ask us to delete your personal information, subject to exceptions such as records we must keep for accounting.
  • Right to correct: ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing: we don't sell or share, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • Right to limit the use of sensitive personal information: we don't collect it.
  • Right to non-discrimination: we won't treat you differently for using any of these rights.

How to make a request

Email office@digitalmovement.uk with the subject "California privacy request" and tell us what you would like us to do. We confirm your identity by matching the details you gave us, for example the name, phone number and email in your form submission. An authorized agent can make a request for you with your written permission; we may still ask you to confirm your identity directly. We respond within 45 days. If we need more time, we tell you why.
